Research Project

Multimodal Deep Learning for Automated Football Highlight Generation using 3D CNN and Whisper Transcription

Under Review
Deep Learning Computer Vision Natural Language Processing Multimodal AI Video Analysis

Research Problem

Manually creating football highlights from a complete match is time-consuming and requires human review of the entire video. This research investigates how multimodal artificial intelligence can automatically identify important football events and generate a concise highlights video.

Proposed Approach

The system combines three complementary sources of information: video, audio, and commentary text. A 3D CNN analyzes temporal visual information, while Whisper converts football commentary into text. These signals are combined using multimodal decision fusion to improve important event detection.

Events Detected

⚽ Goal 🟨 Foul 🥅 Penalty 🚩 Corner 🎯 Free Kick 🟥 Red Card

Research Outcome

The proposed multimodal pipeline automatically identifies significant moments in football matches and uses their timestamps to construct a condensed highlights video. The research demonstrates how computer vision, speech recognition, and multimodal decision fusion can be combined for automated sports video summarization.
